🔥 ROAST MACHINE

Privacy & AI Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · Applies to the Roast Machine iOS app by AechTech

The short version
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The one photo you choose is sent to OpenAI to write the bit, and the written bit is sent to ElevenLabs to voice it. That is the only data that ever leaves your phone.
  • We (AechTech) never see, store, or keep your photos, scripts, or audio. We run no servers of our own.
  • Everything you hear is generated by AI. It is comedy, not fact.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. What the app does

Roast Machine lets you snap or pick a photo, choose a comedian persona, and hear a short roast (or, with the HYPE switch, an over-the-top compliment) spoken out loud. To do that it uses two third-party AI services described below.

3. Third-party AI services

Roast Machine uses third-party generative AI to create its content. Before the first photo is ever sent, the app shows a "Before the Show" screen that explains exactly what goes where and asks for your permission. Nothing is sent unless you tap Let's go. The providers are:

  • OpenAI — receives the photo and writes the script. Under OpenAI's API data policy, API inputs are not used to train their models and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted. See OpenAI's API data usage policy.
  • ElevenLabs — receives only the text of the script and returns audio. See ElevenLabs' privacy policy.

These providers act as processors for the app. We do not send them anything beyond what is listed above.

4. AI content policy

  • It's a bit, not the truth. Every roast and every hype is written by an AI comedian playing a character. Nothing it says is a statement of fact about you or anyone else.
  • Guardrails are built in. Every request instructs the model to keep the material about what's visible in the picture — outfit, hair, pose, background, era — and to never target protected characteristics, weight, disability, or anything cruel. Profanity is capped at "damn".
  • Roast yourself, or with consent. Use photos of yourself, or of friends who are in on the joke. Do not use the app on photos of people who haven't agreed, and never on photos of minors.
  • It can misfire. AI output is unpredictable. If a bit crosses a line, tap TRY AGAIN — and let us know using the contact below so we can tighten the guardrails.
  • Sharing is on you. When you share a roast video, you are publishing AI-generated content about the person in the photo. Share responsibly.

5. Children

Roast Machine is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly process data from them. If you believe a child has used the app with their photo, contact us and we will help; because we store nothing, there is nothing on our side to delete, but we will assist with provider requests.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain no personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Deleting the app removes the on-device flags and cache. Videos you export live in the location you shared them to (Photos, Messages, etc.) and are under your control. Provider retention is described in section 3.

7. Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Photos are downscaled before leaving the device and are never written to disk by the app.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we hold none, most requests will be satisfied by uninstalling the app; for anything else, or to exercise rights with our AI providers, email us and we will help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
